# 📊 GrowthSphere Digital – Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau project developed to evaluate digital marketing campaign effectiveness and understand how marketing investments translate into customer conversions and attributed revenue.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of campaign performance, combining key marketing KPIs, campaign efficiency analysis, regional conversion trends, and customer segment insights.

The objective is to help businesses identify high-performing campaigns, evaluate marketing spend, understand customer acquisition patterns, and make data-driven decisions to improve campaign effectiveness and return on investment.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate the overall effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns.
- Monitor key performance indicators such as CTR, conversion rate, CPC, CPA, and ROAS.
- Identify campaigns generating the highest return on advertising spend.
- Compare marketing expenditure with attributed revenue.
- Analyze conversion events across different customer regions.
- Understand campaign performance across customer segments.
- Identify opportunities to optimize marketing budgets and improve customer acquisition.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The project uses three interconnected datasets:

- **Campaigns:** Contains campaign-level information and campaign identifiers.
- **Conversions:** Contains customer conversion events and attributed revenue information.
- **Daily Performance:** Contains daily campaign metrics such as clicks, impressions, and marketing spend.

The datasets are connected through Campaign ID relationships to enable integrated campaign performance analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Data Modeling** – Establishing relationships between multiple datasets.
- **Calculated Fields** – Creating marketing KPIs and performance metrics.
- **Data Analysis** – Campaign efficiency, customer segmentation, and regional analysis.
- **Interactive Filters** – Exploring campaign performance across different dimensions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive KPI Overview
- Presents the overall performance of marketing campaigns.
- Tracks clicks, conversions, impressions, spending, and attributed revenue.
- Provides a quick overview of campaign efficiency through key marketing ratios.

### 2. Top Campaigns by ROAS
- Identifies the five campaigns generating the highest return on advertising spend.
- Highlights campaigns with stronger revenue generation relative to marketing investment.
- Helps identify potential candidates for further evaluation and scaling.

### 3. Campaign Spend vs Attributed Revenue
- Uses a scatter plot to compare campaign expenditure with attributed revenue.
- Helps understand the relationship between marketing investment and revenue generation.
- Highlights differences in campaign-level financial performance.

### 4. Conversion Events by Region
- Compares conversion events across five customer regions.
- Identifies regions contributing the highest number of conversions.
- Supports region-based marketing strategy development.

### 5. Campaign Performance by Customer Segment
- Compares campaign conversions across three customer segments:
  - New Customers
  - Returning Customers
  - Lapsed Customers
- Helps evaluate customer acquisition and re-engagement performance.

### 6. Insights & Recommendations
A dedicated insights panel summarizes important findings and provides business recommendations related to campaign returns, customer acquisition, and product revenue contribution.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Overall Marketing Returns:** Campaigns generated approximately 3.37x ROAS on ₹1.18 lakh in marketing spend, delivering approximately ₹3.98 lakh in attributed revenue.
- **Top Campaign Efficiency:** Newsletter Campaign 35 achieved the highest ROAS of 11.05x, making it a strong candidate for further evaluation and scaling.
- **Customer Acquisition:** New customers contributed 2,032 conversion events, representing the largest share of conversions.
- **Regional Performance:** East recorded the highest conversion count with 743 conversion events, followed by North with 730.
- **Product Revenue Contribution:** Annual Subscription generated approximately ₹1.66 lakh, the highest attributed revenue among the five product categories.
